Philosophy
My Approach

The focus of this site is to highlight AI-assisted prototypes and demonstrations that I have built using primarily Claude Code CLI or my hosted LLM environment.

You may be asking "Why the focus on AI, and how does it relate to Product Management or Sales Engineer roles?"

My answer is multi-faceted:

  • I strongly believe that AI is the future, and that future is now. One must remain up-to-date on everything related to AI, or risk falling behind the learning curve.
  • AI is transforming how Product Managers and Sales Engineers work.

    As an example, in 2019, would a Product Manager be able to create a functional prototype (or ready-for-production application) in a matter of hours? Or complex Product Requirements Documents in a matter of hours? Likely not.

    The same holds true for a Sales Engineer (SE) or Solutions Architect (SA) role. An example that I frequently encountered as a Sales Engineer: A prospect would like a one-day onsite deep dive whiteboard session to walk through a complex API integration. As an SE I would spend many days preparing for the walkthrough and creating demonstration materials.

    However, with the assistance of AI, the SE/SA now has the ability to build an interactive walkthrough of complex API/integration scenarios in a matter of hours, instead of days. It could be tailored to the prospect's requirements, and built using the same programming language of the prospect. With the end goal of effectively demonstrating live API calls while simultaneously speaking through the details of each step.

    Overall, the benefits to daily productivity and creativity that AI brings to either role only touch the surface of what is possible.

My approach to AI-assisted prototyping is to provide detailed requirements via .MD file, breaking the work into phases rather than a singular approach. I have found this method to be effective, especially when combined with Anthropic-built skills, or open-source skills available in Github.

The common theme in either role is that AI is a game changer in terms of:

  • Translating complexity into simplicity, whether that is in the form of product requirements for engineering teams as a Product Manager, or demonstrating complex topics to prospective clients as an SE/SA.

With the end goal being:

  • As a Product Manager, utilizing the benefits provided by AI to quickly and efficiently launch a successful revenue-producing product.
  • As an SE/SA, utilizing the benefits provided by AI to create effective job aids, leading to increased conversion rates from prospect to actual revenue-generating client for the organization.
